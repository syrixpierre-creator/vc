How it works

From a new contact to a safe backup in four steps

  1. 1

    Create

    Add a contact with several numbers, e-mails, organisation and notes. Numbers are normalised to the international format.

  2. 2

    Save

    It is stored in your private address book. Duplicates are caught before they are saved.

  3. 3

    Sync

    If you connected Google Contacts and kept automatic sync on, it is sent to your own Google account in the background.

  4. 4

    Export

    Download a VCF file at any time, or keep encrypted backups and restore them after a preview.

Security and privacy

Your contacts stay yours

Clear lines about what we store, what goes to Google and what never happens.

What we store

  • The contacts you create or import, in your private address book
  • Sync status and error history, never contact contents in logs
  • Google tokens and payout details, encrypted with AES-256

What goes to Google

  • Sign-in asks only for your name and e-mail
  • Contacts access is a separate permission you grant later, if you want sync
  • Contacts go only to the account you signed in with; nothing while sync is paused

What never happens

  • Your contacts in profiles, leaderboards or reports
  • Selling or sharing address books
  • Messages sent to the people in your contacts
  • Deleting anything in your Google Contacts

You stay in control

  • Pause sync, disconnect Google or revoke access at any time
  • See your active sessions and sign out other devices
  • Download all your data or delete your account

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

Something missing? Write to us and a person will answer.

Contact support
Why only Google sign-in?

It means no password stored here and no password to leak. Your Google account is identified by its stable Google ID; we never see or ask for your Google password.

What does the Google Contacts permission allow?

Sign-in only shares your name and e-mail. If you choose to connect Google Contacts, Google asks you separately to let SYRIX VCF create and update contacts in your own account. You can revoke it from the app or from your Google account at any time.

How does synchronisation work?

Every contact you save is queued and sent in the background to the Google account you authorised. Failed attempts are retried with increasing delays. Deleting a contact here never deletes it in Google, and a conflict is shown to you instead of being overwritten.

Which VCF files can I import?

vCard 2.1, 3.0 and 4.0, up to 5 MB per file. You see a preview first: how many contacts will be added, which ones are duplicates and which records are invalid. Nothing is written until you confirm.

Who counts as a qualified referral?

Someone who signs up through your link with their own verified Google account, keeps the account for at least 24 hours and passes the anti-fraud checks. Clicks, self-referrals and duplicate accounts never count.

When is the $10.00 reward paid?

When your 500th referral qualifies, the reward is created once and a payout request is opened. Payouts are sent manually after review, usually within a few business days. You can follow every status change in your wallet.

What if a payout fails?

The status shows Failed with the reason, and the amount goes back to your available balance. Nothing is shown as paid unless the transfer was confirmed with a reference. You can update your payout details and the team can retry after checking.

How hard are the challenges?

Deliberately hard. Each one needs real, measurable work such as qualified referrals, verified synchronisations or activity on many separate days. Progress is computed by the server, and the terms cannot change once someone has qualified.

Is XP worth money?

No. XP, levels, VIP ranks and badges show your progress and unlock benefits inside the app. They can never be withdrawn or exchanged for money.

How do I delete my account?

In Settings, choose Delete my account. You have 14 days to change your mind; after that your contacts, backups and personal details are erased. Records the law requires, such as completed payments, are kept without your name.

Who can see my contacts?

Only you. Administrators see counts and sync status, never the contents of an address book. Contacts never appear in profiles, leaderboards or shared cards.
