Privacy

Your contacts stay yours

Clear lines about what we store, what we send to Google, and what we never do.

What we store

  • Contacts you create or import
  • Sync status and error history
  • Payout details, encrypted

What goes to Google

  • New contacts, only to the account you authorised
  • Nothing while sync is paused
  • Revoke access whenever you want

What we never do

  • Show contacts in profiles or leaderboards
  • Sell or share address books
  • Message the people in your contacts
  • Use your contacts to target ads

Questions

Why does Google ask for Contacts access?

Only to create and update contacts in the account you authorise. You can pause, disconnect or revoke it, and your local contacts stay.

How does withdrawal work?

Once your balance reaches $15 you can request a withdrawal to PayPal, Binance Pay or Pix. The team reviews it, sends the money, then confirms it. A failed transfer is never shown as paid.

Is there a limit to what I can earn?

The rate applies to qualified referrals only. Reward budgets and anti-fraud review can apply, and the published rules always come first.

How many phone numbers can I save per contact?

One. Each contact holds a single phone number, which keeps your address book simple and easy to sync.

Are there ads?

Ads can appear on public pages and the dashboard overview, always labelled and only after you agree. They never appear on pages that show your contacts, your wallet or payout details, and they never use your contacts. Reward rules do not depend on you clicking ads.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, from settings. You can export your data first.
